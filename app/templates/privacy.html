Batangas Premium operates MEATTRACK for its public website and business portals. This notice explains how we handle personal information about website visitors, invited resellers, portal users, and people whose details are included in an order, return, message, or business record. Batangas Premium is responsible for the information processed through MEATTRACK.

Information We Process

Depending on how you use MEATTRACK, we may process:

  • Names, business names, email addresses, phone numbers, business or delivery addresses, account roles, and profile images.
  • Account, password, one-time verification code, session, and trusted-device records used to create and secure accounts.
  • Cart, order, payment status, payment evidence, delivery, return, replacement, refund, and inventory records.
  • Messages and attachments in signed-in reseller conversations, support requests, notifications, and reports.
  • Records of portal actions, security events, and system activity used for operations, audits, and troubleshooting.
  • Messages sent to the public product-support chatbot. The chatbot does not submit reseller applications; reseller prospects are directed to contact the Business Owner.

How We Use Information

We use information to issue and verify reseller invitations, administer accounts and sign-ins, respond to support questions, manage carts and orders, review payment evidence, coordinate delivery, handle return, replacement and refund workflows, maintain inventory, prepare business reports and forecasts, send account and order notifications, investigate disputes or misuse, protect MEATTRACK, and meet applicable accounting, tax, and legal obligations.

Depending on the activity, processing is based on your consent where we ask for it, steps needed to establish or carry out an account or order arrangement, legal obligations, or legitimate business interests such as operating and securing the service, subject to applicable law. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; this does not affect processing that has another lawful basis.

Chatbot and Service Providers

The public chatbot keeps up to six recent messages, with each message limited to 300 characters, in your MEATTRACK session so it can answer related questions. For some product-support replies, the message and relevant recent chat history may be sent to the configured AI model service, currently accessed through OpenRouter. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, payment details, or other private information in chatbot messages. The chatbot does not create a reseller application or connect visitors to Sales staff.

We use Brevo to deliver account verification, invitation, and other transactional emails. Service providers may process information needed to provide hosting, database, email, or AI services to us. They are expected to handle it for those services and under applicable safeguards. We may provide relevant order or contact details to delivery partners when needed to arrange delivery, and disclose records to professional advisers, auditors, or authorities when necessary or required by law.

Access and Security

Portal access is restricted by role and assigned work. Owners can review business-wide records. Staff access is limited to their work and assigned responsibilities. Resellers can access their own account, orders, and related conversations. Private evidence and messages are available only to authorized participants and staff. We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Retention

We retain account, order, payment, return, message, report, and audit records for as long as they are needed to operate the business, resolve disputes, maintain security, meet accounting or legal obligations, or protect legitimate business interests. Some temporary security and verification records are removed through scheduled cleanup after about 30 days; expired session records are cleaned up after expiry. Historical visitor live-chat messages are cleaned up 30 days after a conversation is closed or cancelled. MEATTRACK does not currently define one deletion period for all business records. When information is no longer needed, it is deleted, anonymized, or securely restricted as appropriate and as permitted by law.

Your Privacy Rights and Contact

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether we process your personal information, request access or correction, object to or seek restriction of processing, request deletion or blocking where allowed, request portability where applicable, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. Requests and privacy questions may be sent to Batangas Premium at pat.clem.inc@gmail.com or 0935 335 6483. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and some records may need to be retained where the law or a legitimate business need requires it.
